1950 · A living coastal story

Where heritage
meets the horizon.

Changi Beach Club has a distinctive history along Singapore's eastern shore. This preview uses its documented heritage as a design reference.

The clubhouse

A place with a story.

The site traces its beginnings to the RAF Officers' Club in 1950, later becoming Changi Swimming Club and taking its present name in 1988.

Our design direction honours that identity without suggesting the new PLP chapter is already affiliated with the club.
